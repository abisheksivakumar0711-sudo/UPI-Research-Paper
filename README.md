Research on UPI adoption, digital payment behavior, and QR-code preference patterns using UTAUT framework and statistical analysis
# Digital Payment Governance Research

## Overview
This research project analyzes urban users’ perceptions of Unified Payments Interface (UPI) usage in Chennai, India, with a focus on digital payment adoption, QR-code preferences, and demographic usage behavior.

The study applies the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT) framework together with statistical analysis methods to evaluate factors influencing digital payment adoption.

## Research Focus
The project explores:
- Digital payment adoption behavior
- UPI usage patterns
- QR-code payment preferences
- Technology acceptance factors
- Urban digital governance
- Financial inclusion and accessibility

## Methodology
- Cross-sectional survey research
- Quantitative data analysis
- Mann-Whitney U Test
- Ordinal Logistic Regression
- UTAUT Framework application

## Key Findings
- No statistically significant difference in UPI usage frequency between students and working professionals
- Convenience, speed, and perceived security significantly influence QR-code scanning preference
- QR-code payments are strongly associated with ease of use and operational efficiency
